# Referensi

Referensi dibagi menjadi tiga jenis agar jelas apa yang menjadi **bukti ilmiah**,
apa yang menjadi **rujukan API**, dan apa yang hanya **bacaan pengantar**.
Setiap halaman materi mengutip subset yang relevan dengan klaimnya.

!!! note "Catatan verifikasi"
    Daftar ini disusun dari pengetahuan penulis dan belum seluruhnya diperiksa
    ulang terhadap sumber daring. Periksa DOI/URL sebelum mengutip dalam karya
    ilmiah. Status pemeriksaan dicatat di [Audit dan Verifikasi](lampiran/audit-verifikasi.md).

## Referensi akademik (primer)

### Representasi teks dan klasifikasi klasik

- Spärck Jones, K. (1972). A statistical interpretation of term specificity and its application in retrieval. *Journal of Documentation*, 28(1), 11–21. (asal-usul IDF)
- Salton, G., & Buckley, C. (1988). Term-weighting approaches in automatic text retrieval. *Information Processing & Management*, 24(5), 513–523.
- McCallum, A., & Nigam, K. (1998). A comparison of event models for Naive Bayes text classification. *AAAI-98 Workshop on Learning for Text Categorization*.
- Joachims, T. (1998). Text categorization with Support Vector Machines: Learning with many relevant features. *ECML 1998*.
- Pedregosa, F., dkk. (2011). Scikit-learn: Machine learning in Python. *Journal of Machine Learning Research*, 12, 2825–2830.

### Evaluasi dan keandalan eksperimen

- Fawcett, T. (2006). An introduction to ROC analysis. *Pattern Recognition Letters*, 27(8), 861–874.
- Saito, T., & Rehmsmeier, M. (2015). The precision-recall plot is more informative than the ROC plot when evaluating binary classifiers on imbalanced datasets. *PLoS ONE*, 10(3), e0118432.
- Kaufman, S., Rosset, S., Perlich, C., & Stitelman, O. (2012). Leakage in data mining: Formulation, detection, and avoidance. *ACM TKDD*, 6(4).
- Guo, C., Pleiss, G., Sun, Y., & Weinberger, K. Q. (2017). On calibration of modern neural networks. *ICML 2017*.

### Deep learning dan model bahasa

- Rumelhart, D. E., Hinton, G. E., & Williams, R. J. (1986). Learning representations by back-propagating errors. *Nature*, 323, 533–536.
- Hochreiter, S., & Schmidhuber, J. (1997). Long short-term memory. *Neural Computation*, 9(8), 1735–1780.
- Cho, K., dkk. (2014). Learning phrase representations using RNN encoder–decoder for statistical machine translation. *EMNLP 2014*. arXiv:1406.1078.
- Bahdanau, D., Cho, K., & Bengio, Y. (2015). Neural machine translation by jointly learning to align and translate. *ICLR 2015*. arXiv:1409.0473.
- Mikolov, T., Chen, K., Corrado, G., & Dean, J. (2013). Efficient estimation of word representations in vector space. arXiv:1301.3781.
- Pennington, J., Socher, R., & Manning, C. D. (2014). GloVe: Global vectors for word representation. *EMNLP 2014*.
- Bojanowski, P., Grave, E., Joulin, A., & Mikolov, T. (2017). Enriching word vectors with subword information. *TACL*, 5, 135–146.
- Vaswani, A., dkk. (2017). Attention is all you need. *NeurIPS 2017*. arXiv:1706.03762.
- Devlin, J., Chang, M.-W., Lee, K., & Toutanova, K. (2019). BERT: Pre-training of deep bidirectional transformers for language understanding. *NAACL-HLT 2019*. arXiv:1810.04805.
- Wilie, B., dkk. (2020). IndoNLU: Benchmark and resources for evaluating Indonesian natural language understanding. *AACL-IJCNLP 2020*. arXiv:2009.05387.
- Koto, F., Rahimi, A., Lau, J. H., & Baldwin, T. (2020). IndoLEM and IndoBERT: A benchmark dataset and pre-trained language model for Indonesian NLP. *COLING 2020*. arXiv:2011.00677.

### Bahasa Indonesia, media sosial, dan kebencanaan

### Interpretabilitas, bias, dan dokumentasi
